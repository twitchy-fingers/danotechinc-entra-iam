How this app decides what you can do

This app has no user list of its own. When you sign in, Microsoft Entra ID issues an ID token containing your app roles. Roles are assigned to groups, and the groups are populated automatically from your HR record (job code, department, whether you manage people). Change jobs in HR, and your access here changes at your next sign-in.
